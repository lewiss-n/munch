WHO MANJI IS

A KENYAN FOOD COMPANY BUILT AROUND BISCUITS & BAKED SNACKS.

Manji Food Industries develops, manufactures and distributes food products for everyday snacking. Our portfolio spans biscuits and baked snacks made for different tastes, occasions and preferences.

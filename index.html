The Manji story

From a Ngara bakery to a Kenyan biscuit icon.

Manji's official history traces the business back to humble beginnings as a bakery in Ngara, Nairobi, where it manufactured bread. The bakery flourished before wheat rationing disrupted that early chapter. What followed became one of Kenya's most recognisable biscuit stories.

01

Humble beginnings

A bakery in Ngara

House of Manji began as a bread bakery in Ngara. It built a growing local business before wheat rationing made the bakery business increasingly difficult.

02

1954

The biscuit legacy takes shape

The Manji name became closely associated with biscuit manufacturing in Kenya, building a tradition that would stretch across generations and become part of everyday tea-time culture.

03

November 2002

A new chapter under Manji Food Industries

Manji Food Industries Limited took over the mantle in November 2002 under the chairmanship of Dr. H. P. Dawda, beginning a renewed chapter for the company and its much-loved biscuit portfolio.

The legacy today

More than seven decades around the table.

Across biscuits, cookies, wafers and cakes, Manji continues a heritage built around familiar favourites, consistent quality and products shared by families across generations.
